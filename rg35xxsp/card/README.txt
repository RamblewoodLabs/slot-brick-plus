Slot for the Anbernic RG35XX SP.

TF1 is BaseOS. This card is TF2. BaseOS starts System/launch_frontend.sh,
which runs Slot/System/slot.

Games, saves and settings are inside Slot/, in the same folders as a TrimUI
Brick card: Games, BIOS, Saves, States, Labels, Config.

Do not format TF1 after BaseOS is flashed. Full steps are in docs/rg35xxsp.md
in the source repository.
