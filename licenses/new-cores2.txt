The cores added for ColecoVision, Atari 5200, Atari 800, Vectrex, 32X, Nintendo DS,
Nintendo 64 and PSP. Each one's source, at the commit it was built from, is in vendor/
(<core>-src.tar.gz, checked by brick/cores-systems2.sha256 and
brick/gpu-cores/sources.sha256); brick/build-cores-systems2.sh and
brick/build-gpu-cores.sh build them from it.

GPL-2.0 is the text Slot already ships (fceumm-GPL-2.0.txt); GPL-3.0 is in GPL-3.0.txt.
PicoDrive is under its own license, in picodrive-COPYING.txt: it may be redistributed,
not sold or used commercially, and a modified build must come with its complete source.
Slot's build of it is unmodified and its source is in vendor/picodrive-src.tar.gz.

PPSSPP's own files (fonts, shaders, compat.ini, the PPGe atlas) go on the card in
BIOS/PPSSPP, from vendor/ppsspp-assets; they are part of PPSSPP and under its license.

DraStic, the usual DS emulator on these handhelds, is closed and may not be redistributed,
so the DS shelf runs melonDS.
